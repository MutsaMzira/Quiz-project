How To Play

Here's a quick guide on how the BrainBlast quiz works.

01

Start The Quiz

Press the Start Quiz button on the home page. Every game loads questions in a different random order.

02

Answer Questions

The quiz contains different question formats.

  • Multiple Choice Pick one answer from the choices given.
  • True / False Decide if the statement is true or false.
  • Fill in Blank Type your own answer into the box.
03

Check Feedback

After answering, the system immediately shows whether your answer was correct or incorrect.

04

Move To The Next Question

Use the Next button to continue through the quiz. The progress bar helps track how far you are.

05

View Results

At the end of the quiz you will see your final score, percentage and answer summary.

06

Try Again

You can replay the quiz as many times as you want and try to improve your score.

Scoring System

90 - 100% 🏆 Excellent
70 - 89% 🥈 Very Good
50 - 69% 🥉 Average
0 - 49% 📚 Keep Practicing
